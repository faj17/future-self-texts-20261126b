# Texts From Your Future Self

A funny text message from your future self, in 111 characters, for make.fun's "Texts From Your Future Self" bounty. `main.py` writes the text to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Future you. That unread email from your dentist? I just paid $4,200 for the tooth. Open the email. Open it now.

The unread email is the setup everyone recognises; the $4,200 tooth is the bill for ignoring it, and the doubled "Open it now" is the future self shouting through the phone.
